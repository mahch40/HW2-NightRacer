## Made by me

- All C# scripts.
- Lane dashes, coin, fuel can, car neon bars and glow pools, and the glowing bulbs on the street lamps (built from Unity primitives and materials).
- The heart icon (drawn by a one-time editor script) and the horizon fade texture (generated the same way).
- Particle effects (fireworks, exhaust, crash sparks, pickup sparkle), particle and glow materials, the ground plane, and the Bloom, fog and lighting setup.

## Tools

Unity 6 with the Universal Render Pipeline, the Input System package and TextMesh Pro, all from Unity Technologies.
